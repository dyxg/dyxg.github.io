Xuanyi Zhou is a research intern in UC Berkeley’s Sky Computing Lab, where he works with [Prof. Joseph E. Gonzalez](https://people.eecs.berkeley.edu/~jegonzal/) on reinforcement learning for language model agents. He earned a B.E. in Software Engineering from Zhejiang University, where his advisor was [Prof. Can Wang](https://person.zju.edu.cn/en/wangcan). Previously, he worked on faster sampling for diffusion models in [Prof. Siwei Lyu](https://cse.buffalo.edu/~siweilyu/)’s lab at the University at Buffalo.

He led [EasyPPO](https://easyppo.github.io/), a project that identified two sources of instability in reinforcement learning for language models and addressed both by changing how the critic is trained. He is a first author of [SVG-EAR](https://svg-project.github.io/v3/) (ECCV 2026), a sparse attention method for video generation that works without retraining the model. He also contributed to [FrontierCS 2](https://frontier-cs.org/), a benchmark that tests whether coding agents can outperform the code released with published computer science papers.

**I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027.**
